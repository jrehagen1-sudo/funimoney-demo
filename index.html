    Demo rules

    • Roll 2 dice and move clockwise. Passing START (Zoo Station) is one lap and pays 2, plus 1 for each border flag you hold. Finish the laps without losing all your coins.
    • West half: pay by printed value; the bank gives change. East half: every coin counts as 1, taken from the top of your stack.
    • Paying a player on the other half: they roll a die for each 1 owed and take that coin, counted from the top.
    • At Checkpoint Charlie and Brandenburg Gate you may re-order your stack. New coins go on top.
    • Shortcuts (gold edge at Potsdamer Platz and Friedrichstrasse): 1 die, every square is a risk, and you stop where you rejoin.
    • Gold: buy at START for 10, never taken from you, cashed in automatically to keep you from going out.
    • The cards are placeholders until the Card Writer bot drafts the real decks.
